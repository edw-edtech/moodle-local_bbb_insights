## 1. Overview

**BigBlueButton Insights** is provided as a free, open-source Moodle local plugin distributed under the terms of the GNU General Public License v3.0 (GPLv3).

- Includes real-time and historical virtual classroom session tracking, participant attendance monitoring, recording quota enforcement, secure recording download protection, and reporting utilities.
- Operates entirely on the local Moodle server and does not require any paid subscription, proprietary license keys, or third-party cloud services.
